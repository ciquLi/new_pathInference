Argument and style changes
1. The introduction now develops the source-path requirement of individual
   inference models, the cold-start problem, and the observed-tail example
   before explaining the design. It connects prefix construction, whole-path
   admission and generation consistency as successive problems.
2. The related work explicitly credits PA-GMPI's source-dependent generator,
   observed-prefix reuse, graph-kernel warm-starting and real-path embedding.
   PathVeil's focus is constructing target-source training evidence from other
   origins; the GMPI scoring architecture and objective remain inherited.
3. The method explains what each stage receives, why its rule is used, and
   what it returns. It distinguishes the retained tree from the admitted
   library and the admission classifier from the downstream route scorer.
4. Repeated probability/forwarding disclaimers were consolidated. Data-role
   and provenance qualifications remain where needed to define the claims.
   Formal research prose replaces checklist-like narration and repeated
   challenge/response labels. No ToN prose or results were copied.
5. The evaluation separates identical AS sets (reported ESR) from ordered
   library-sequence support (CPS). It explains the fixed query denominator,
   the AS328840 failure case, and the interpretation of oracle versus final
   accuracy. It distinguishes a PG generator ablation from an end-to-end
   PA-GMPI comparison.

Remaining evidence and historical-document gaps
1. Inherited graph/AS features lack complete origin-level provenance. The
   draft states path-label isolation without claiming fully source-independent
   construction of all derived features.
2. Ten target sources per month, with extensive references, do not establish
   generalization to arbitrary source topologies or sparsely observed ASes.
3. Existing results support exploration beyond the first hop and the fixed-
   library generator comparison. They do not show a consistent accuracy
   advantage of conditional over full recursion or a measured cost saving.
4. Quality-admission and individual feature-group ablations, novel-join-only
   support, and current-method data-plane validation remain separate empirical
   work. Text was not used to invent those missing results.
5. REVISION_NOTES.txt and RESULT_PROVENANCE.json are historical 2026-10-29
   audits. They cover different/earlier run identities and only three months,
   while the active evaluation now covers five. They were not relabeled as
   current evidence. This writing revision does not certify the raw provenance
   of every baseline or ablation result in the existing tables.
6. The unchanged pseudocode leaves empty-pool max implicit, and its pool-smaller-
   than-B branch assigns the whole pool while the comment says positive-scored
   paths. These are pre-existing specification details; the manuscript prose
   retains the stated positive-score policy, and no numerical method was changed.
