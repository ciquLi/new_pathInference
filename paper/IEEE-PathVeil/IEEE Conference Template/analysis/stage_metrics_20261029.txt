ordered_support_on_reference_pairs: Admitted unique ordered paths exactly matching a reference path, divided by admitted unique ordered paths whose source/destination pair occurs in the references.
unsupported_is_not_false: A path absent from finite reference observations is unobserved; not proven unreal or forwarding-invalid.
no_reversal: No path reversal, completion, or graph inference used in this audit.
