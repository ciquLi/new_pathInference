Scientific limits retained in the draft
- Reused dated graph and AS representation artifacts do not have complete
  original source-withholding provenance. Current label isolation does not
  retrospectively certify those derived features.
- Ordered observation support is a finite-reference metric, not proof of
  forwarding reality. Unknown relationships remain unknown.
- Full-path support includes intact known suffix reuse. Novel-join-only
  support has not been computed and is not claimed.
- Source-relative admission does not guarantee branch representation.
  AS328840 support is poor even when selected first-hop coverage is high.
- The independent domain-endpoint measurement dataset is retained, but no
  current PATS traceroute result is claimed.
- Matched-query/evidence KnownPath and PathRadar baselines and component
  ablations remain to be completed/audited. No old figures are relabeled.
